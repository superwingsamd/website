Programmes

Four ways to serve.

Choose the track that fits you. Every track gets a personal portal, hour logging and coordinator verification.

V

General volunteer

For anyone 16+ who wants to give time regularly or for a single event.

  • Event and field volunteering
  • Verified hours statement
U

University service learning

For students whose university partners with us for credit-bearing community engagement.

  • Institution-linked profile
  • Hours your faculty can rely on
DP

IB Diploma: CAS

Plan and record creativity, activity and service experiences and a CAS project with us.

  • Tag CAS strands and learning outcomes
  • Reflections for your CAS portfolio
CP

IBCP: Community engagement

Career-related Programme students can complete their community engagement (formerly service learning) with us, with documented reflections.

  • Log each stage with reflections
  • Verified by our coordinators
How it works

From sign-up to verified hours.

Sign up

Create your volunteer account below and choose your programme.

Get approved

A coordinator reviews your application and welcomes you in.

Serve

Join projects and events with our team in the community.

Log & reflect

Record each activity, your hours and your reflection in the portal.

Get verified

Coordinators verify entries. Download your hours statement anytime.

For IB students

Built for CAS and community engagement.

When you log an activity as an IB Diploma student, you can tag the CAS strands it covers and the learning outcomes you showed. You can also write a reflection. IBCP students can log their community engagement (formerly service learning) in the same way. Your school's CAS or community engagement coordinator gets a clean, verified record.

CreativityActivityService

Your school's CAS coordinator still decides how your experiences count towards the programme. We give you a verified record of what you did with us.

The seven CAS learning outcomes

LO1Identify own strengths and develop areas for growth
LO2Demonstrate that challenges have been undertaken, developing new skills
LO3Demonstrate how to initiate and plan a CAS experience
LO4Show commitment to and perseverance in CAS experiences
LO5Demonstrate the skills and recognise the benefits of working collaboratively
LO6Demonstrate engagement with issues of global significance
LO7Recognise and consider the ethics of choices and actions
