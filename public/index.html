About us

A trust built to serve, and built to last.

Superwings Foundation was set up in Ahmedabad in 2021 as an irrevocable public charitable trust. Its founder, Manushi Digesh Gajjar, gave the trust its first funds from her own savings. The trust is registered with the Office of the Charity Commissioner, Gujarat, under the Public Trusts Act, 1950.

Our trust deed sets out a simple aim: help every person grow intellectually, morally, physically and culturally, and make sure help reaches the people who need it most. In practice, that means classrooms and libraries, skill and computer training for students and women, health camps, and relief when disaster strikes.

Our main focus is education, literacy and skill development. We also work in health, social welfare, environment and rural development.

Our work

Six areas, one purpose.

Our trust deed lays out six areas where Superwings Foundation works. Each one aims at a fairer, healthier and more capable society.

Education & Literacy

Spreading education so that every citizen can read, learn and grow.

  • Schools, balwadis, anganwadis and night classes
  • Notebooks, books, scholarships and prizes for students in need
  • Libraries, reading rooms and student magazines
  • Adult education, literacy drives and de-addiction awareness
  • Seminars, knowledge camps and educational tours

Skills & Women's Empowerment

Practical skills that lead to jobs and independence.

  • Computer education and tuition classes for students
  • Free training in tailoring, embroidery, typing and computers for women
  • Vocational and employment training centres
  • Girls' education and support for widowed, elderly and destitute women

Health & Medical Aid

Making medical help reachable for every section of society.

  • Health check-up camps and blood donation drives
  • Subsidised treatment and medicines for poor patients
  • Dispensaries, health centres and ambulance support
  • Tiffin service for patients, plus hygiene and disease awareness

Social Welfare

Working toward social justice and better livelihoods.

  • Removing social evils and harmful customs
  • Linking people to government self-employment schemes
  • Community halls, wells, water tanks and drinking-water points
  • Support for families during last rites

Disaster Relief

Standing with people and animals in times of crisis.

  • Relief during floods, earthquakes, famine and epidemics
  • Food, clothing and essentials for affected families
  • Fodder, food, medicine and cattle camps for animals during drought

Environment & Rural Development

Building greener cities and stronger villages.

  • Tree plantation, nurseries and pollution control
  • Sanitation and cleanliness drives across Gujarat
  • Check dams, ponds and watershed work for water security
  • Drinking-water projects with government and local bodies
